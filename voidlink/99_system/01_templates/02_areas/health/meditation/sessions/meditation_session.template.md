### Main Practice ([duration - 4] min)
**Technique:** {{technique}}

**For Breath Meditation:**
- Focus on natural breath
- Count breaths (1-10)
- Notice sensations at nose/belly
- Return attention when mind wanders

**For Body Scan:**
- Systematic attention through body
- Start at feet, move to head
- Notice sensations without judgment
- Release tension as you scan

**For Loving-Kindness:**
- Generate feelings of warmth
- Direct to: self → loved one → neutral → difficult → all beings
- Use phrases: "May [I/you/they] be happy, healthy, peaceful"
